Classic Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup of milk

Instructions

Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
Add the milk and eggs, then stir until smooth.
Cook on a hot griddle until golden brown on both sides.
